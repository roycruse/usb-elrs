This arduino sketch allows an ELRS receiver and a Seed Xiao nrf52840 to work as a elrs to HID reciever for your pc or laptop. 

It can be left permanently plugged in and will just work whenever you turn on your transmitter. 

This code is optimised for the Radiomaster GX12 enabling all buttons and sliders to work. 

Ive also included images to show the soldering between the two boards.

Ive used a tiny foxeer lite elrs receiver as no external antenna is needed in the confines of playing a game or simulator.

I will design and add a 3d printed enclosure in due course.
